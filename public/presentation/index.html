The pattern

Complexity doesn't monetize. Loops and social pressure do.

Loop

60-second compulsion

Plant → wait → harvest → buy. One-thumb, mobile-first, instantly re-playable.

Social

Other players are the content

Stealing, trading, showing off. The core verb involves someone else — free retention.

Scarcity

Rarity & FOMO

Limited eggs, timed events, tradeable pets. Adopt Me turned scarcity into years of play.

Trend

Ride the meme

Kids arrive already wanting the theme. YouTube & TikTok become free marketing.

Idle

Offline progression

It grows while they're logged off — a guaranteed reason to come back tomorrow.

Monetize

Repeat-purchase products

Dev products > game passes. Only 1–3% pay, so build rarity ladders for the whales.

Risks · the honest version

This is a hit-driven, power-law business. We treat it like one.

Risk

Most games earn ~nothing

Assume 3–4 of 5 return under $500. The model already prices this in.

Risk

Platform dependency

Roblox controls discovery, rates & rules. A 2026 policy change cut 12M DAUs in a quarter.

Risk

Cashout delay

30K-Robux minimum + ~monthly hold. First cash is 60–90 days out — a timing risk, not a loss.

Risk

IP / moderation

Meme games flirt with copyright. Mitigation: original characters in a trend-adjacent style.

Risk

Discovery algorithm

It rewards games that already retain. Mitigation: soft-launch, measure D1/D7, then spend.

Risk

Freelancer variance

Quality & ghosting. Mitigation: milestone pay, IP-assignment contracts, small paid test tasks first.

The gap

Where the opening is right now.

  • The 18+ premium nobody optimizes for. Roblox's new US-adult DevEx rate pays +42%, and adults already monetize 50%+ higher than under-18s — a segment almost no simple-game maker targets.
  • Trend windows stay open for weeks. Fast followers of a viral format ("Steal a ___", "Grow a ___") captured real revenue in 2025. Speed is the moat.
  • Under-served lanes: idle/incremental with deep trading, and casual co-op survival — 99 Nights in the Forest proved the co-op lane in 2025 with 17B+ visits.

Our edge isn't a better engine. It's speed, discipline, and buying enough shots to catch a window.
