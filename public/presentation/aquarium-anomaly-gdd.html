Game Design Document

Aquarium Anomaly: two routes

The same game, built two ways. Route A is the cheap observation build. Route B adds a real job on top, in the Animal Hospital mold. Pick one, or start with A and grow into B. Each has its own full design document below.

Route A

Hallway version

$600 to $1,000

Patrol the loop, spot what is wrong, report it before the timer. Pure observation, no upkeep work. Cheapest and fastest to ship.

  • Cheapest build, 2 to 3 weeks
  • Simple to learn
  • Thinner loop, closer to the saturated sub-genre
  • Lower expected retention
Read Route A Route B

Worker-job version recommended

$1,350 to $2,200

Do a real aquarium job (clean, feed, repair) while anomalies hide in the work. The Animal Hospital model, which is what actually stuck on Roblox.

  • Active work, scares land harder
  • Stickier, higher expected retention
  • Costs more and touches more systems
  • Has an MVP floor you can ship first

Our read: Route B is the better game and the one that matches what is actually winning on Roblox right now, but it does not fit the original $600 to $1,000 budget. The clean middle path is to build Route B's MVP (about $1,350 to $1,600), which ships the worker fantasy and the anchor tasks, then patch in the deferred pieces from the first month of revenue. Route A stays on the table as the true low-budget option if the ceiling is hard.
